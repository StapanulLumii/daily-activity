## Fun Fact

Octopuses have three hearts: two pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body. When an octopus swims, the heart that delivers blood to the body actually stops beating, which is why octopuses prefer crawling rather than swimming — it exhausts them less.
